Guardrails

  • Never email. The agent only logs and drafts.
  • No action on existing customers without their account manager.
  • Anything below 0.70 confidence goes to human review.
  • Student emails, competitor domains and unmapped countries always go to human review.
  • Every decision is logged with its reasoning and can be reversed by a person.

Routing rules

Commercial200 to 999 employees: Commercial SDR
Enterprise1,000 to 4,999 employees: Enterprise SDR
Strategic5,000 or more employees: Strategic SDR
Existing customerAccount manager, never an SDR
Two possible accountsHuman review

Data sources

  • Web form webhook, received through Boomi: new lead details.
  • Clay waterfall: person and company enrichment.
  • Salesforce API: accounts, owners, open renewals.
  • Claude: scoring, routing reasoning and draft actions.
  • Territory map: which countries belong to which team.
