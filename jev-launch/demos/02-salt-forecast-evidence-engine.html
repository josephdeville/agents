About this demo

A concept demo of a forecast evidence gate, built by Joe Deville for the Revenue Operations Manager role at Salt Security. The idea: a deal's CRM stage is a claim, the conversation record is the evidence, and the forecast only trusts claims with evidence behind them.

About the builder. Joe Deville: 2 years as owner/admin of a Salesforce org (flows, permission sets, reports and dashboards, Data Loader migrations, dedupe). Led HubSpot-to-Salesforce migrations. About 3 years in RevOps. Previously GTM Engineer at Cortex, building agent workflows. Built LangChain agents at Ignyte for forecasting and lead qualification (30% improvement).

Stack. Clay, HubSpot, Salesforce, Gong, Trigger.dev, LangChain/CrewAI, Smartlead, Neon, Vercel.
